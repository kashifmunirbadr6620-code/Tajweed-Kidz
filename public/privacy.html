1. Commitment to Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Tajweed Kids is designed for children learning Quranic pronunciation and Tajweed rules. Protecting children's online privacy is our utmost priority. We strictly adhere to the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses are uploaded or stored on remote servers.
  • Child profile names, streaks, and stars are stored strictly locally on the user's device.
  • No user accounts or logins are required to use the application.

3. Microphone & Audio Usage

Tajweed Kids includes an interactive "Listen & Repeat" practice mode that requires microphone access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO). This permission is used exclusively on the local device to allow the child to record their recitation and compare it against the teacher's pronunciation. The recorded audio:

  • Is never transmitted over the internet.
  • Is never stored permanently or uploaded to any server or cloud service.
  • Is discarded as soon as the practice session ends or the app closes.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Trackers

Tajweed Kids is 100% ad-free. There are no third-party advertisements, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and no marketing SDKs.
